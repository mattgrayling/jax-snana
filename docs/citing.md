# Citing SNANA and jax-snana

jax-snana is of course heavily reliant on the SNANA simulator, and so we
request that you cite SNANA when using jax-snana,
[Kessler et al. (2009)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2009PASP..121.1028K),
as well as jax-snana itself,
[Grayling (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19000).

When you use one of the models within jax-SNANA, we request that you also
cite the original source; see the table below for the appropriate citations
for each model.

| Model | Citations |
|---|---|
| SALT2 | [Guy et al. (2007)](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0701828) |
| SALT3 / SALT3.K21 | [Kenworthy et al. (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.07795) |
| SALT3.Dovekie | [Kenworthy et al. (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.07795) and [Popovic et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05471) |
| BayeSN G26 | [Mandel et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07538) and [Grayling et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19429) |
| BayeSN W22 | [Mandel et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07538) and [Ward et al. (2023)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023ApJ...956..111W/abstract) |
| redback-jax models | [Sarin et al. (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12806) and the papers for the individual models, listed in the [Redback documentation](https://redback.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) |
| User-supplied spectral templates or model grids | The publications describing the templates or models used. |
